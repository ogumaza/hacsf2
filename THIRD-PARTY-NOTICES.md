## Console data

Nothing in hacsf2 licenses game data. The sound archives that hacsf2 reads belong to their copyright holders.

ntdlib's `include/ntdlib/nwsnd/tables.h` contains the numeric tables of NintendoWare's sound libraries: powers of
two, decibel levels, envelope attack multipliers, pan curves, low-pass cutoffs, biquad filter coefficients and sines.
The models use the exact values to reproduce the consoles' output. The files `src/sequence_export/filter_tables_*.cpp`
of ntdlib contain the filters of each console, fitted to SoundFont 2 and SFZ settings.
