A maintenance release. hacsf2 converts every sound archive whose sounds have names to the same files as 1.1.1.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `hacsf2-1.1.2-win-x64.zip` | Windows x64 executable, `README.md`, `LICENSE` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` |

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop one or more `.bfsar` files onto `hacsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `hacsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each archive.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Hacsf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## Changes

- **Sounds without names.** A sequence sound that has no name in the archive is named after the label where it starts in its sequence file, without `_Begin`, in place of `SEQ_<n>`. The warning about a damaged name table says so.
- **The README.** Three corrections. Super Mario Party Jamboree's procedure tables are `00.msgpack` to `15.msgpack`, and hacsf2 also finds them for an archive in `_Resident`. Only a `--seed` other than the default turns off the search for a later start. ctest also runs hacsf2's tests of the profiles, the sounding starts and the procedure table. The rest of the README, the notices and the earlier release notes use plainer wording.
- **The notices.** `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` now lists every part of xsflib and ntdlib that hacsf2 uses. The parts that it left out are the SHA-1 hashes, the UTF-8 manifest of the Windows program, the games' rules for their archives, the reader of Bezel Engine's sound sheets, and the models of the games' user procedures, rhythm modes and music sync.
- **The libraries.** hacsf2 now builds on seqlib 1.5.0, xsflib 1.12.0 and ntdlib 7.0.0.
