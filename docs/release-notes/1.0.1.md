A bug-fix release: loops, held endings, sweeps and pitch LFOs now follow the game more closely. A player that loops on the markers keeps the loop's tempo. Convert an archive again to get the corrected files.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `hacsf2-1.0.1-win-x64.zip` | Windows x64 executable, `README.md`, `LICENSE` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` |

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. The executable is now built with clang-cl instead of MSVC. Drop one or more `.bfsar` files onto `hacsf2.exe`, or use a terminal. `hacsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each archive.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. Linux and Intel Macs are no longer supported. macOS builds include `Hacsf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## Fixes

- **Loops within loops.** A loop that its track jumps back to during a pass of another loop counts as an inner loop. Its passes no longer make it the main loop. The loop markers now span a whole pass of the outer loop, with its notes. The game repeats that pass. The markers used to sit on a short inner loop, sometimes a single silent tick.
- **Counted loops.** A sequence that the 15-minute limit cuts inside a counted loop is performed again with that loop endless. It gets loop markers and ends after the usual repeats. Music that a game plays as a counted loop of many passes used to come out cut at 15 minutes.
- **Loop tempo.** The tempo changes on a loop's first tick come after the `loopStart` marker and CC111. A player that loops on the markers now plays each repeat at the tempo of the loop's start. Playback without looping is unchanged.
- **Tracks that wait forever.** In the game, a track that waits a negative number of ticks stops there for good. Such a wait comes from a wait or note length taken from a variable left at -1. hacsf2 now stops such a track too. A later track's loop can then take the loop markers. A sequence whose tracks all stop that way ends 10 seconds after its last change. Such sequences used to run to the 15-minute limit.
- **Fades in held endings.** A fade of the second volume or the main volume counts as a change. A sequence that holds until the game stops it now ends 10 seconds after such a fade finishes. A fade longer than 10 seconds used to be cut short.
- **Pitch LFOs that change during a note.** Such a note plays on a free MIDI channel. Its pitch bends follow the game's LFO frame by frame, curve and phase included. The note used to keep the LFO settings of its start.
- **Sweeps below hearing.** A track's pitch bends follow only the sweeps and pitch LFOs of notes that can still be heard. A newer note used to follow the sweep of an older note's release tail 40 dB down. Its pitch was often several semitones off. A note shorter than one 5 ms sound frame now keeps its sweep and pitch LFO in the frames between ticks.
- **Notes in one tick.** A track that starts more than 96 notes in one tick now keeps up to 1,024 of them. hacsf2 used to keep the first 96.
- **Reports.** A random LFO curve in a note's pitch bends is reported. Its numbers can differ from those in the game. A pitch LFO that changes when no MIDI channel is free is reported too.

## Other changes

- hacsf2 is built on three libraries: seqlib, ntdlib and xsflib. They are git submodules. Clone with `git clone --recursive`, or run `git submodule update --init` in a checkout. `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` gives their licence texts. The Windows zip includes it.
