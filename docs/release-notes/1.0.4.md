A release that brings the SoundFonts and SFZ files closer to their specifications. Attenuated zones now play at the game's level in FluidSynth and BASSMIDI. Convert an archive again to get the corrected files.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop one or more `.bfsar` files onto `hacsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `hacsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each archive.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Hacsf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## Fixes

- **Attenuation.** FluidSynth, BASSMIDI and the E-mu chips apply 0.4 of a zone's initial attenuation. The specification calls for all of it. hacsf2 now writes 2.5 times a zone's attenuation in the generator, up to the generator's limit of 1440 cB. A modulator without a source holds any attenuation beyond 57.6 dB. In those players, a region at volume 64 used to play 2.4 dB below one at 127. In the game, it plays 6 dB below. A player that follows the specification now plays attenuated zones too quietly.
- **Pan range.** The specification gives the default CC10 modulator an amount of 1000. Most players apply 500. Each instrument's global zone now holds a CC10 modulator of amount 500 in place of the default one. A player that follows the specification then pans as the others do.
- **SFZ decays and releases.** The SFZ files now follow the curve that the documentation at sfzformat.com gives. A decay or release falls by a factor of e^-8 over its time. That's 69.49 dB. As in the game, the fall is linear in dB. The files used to give the times for sfizz's faster curve. sfizz 1.2.3 falls by a factor of e^-9. Where the game has fallen 40 dB, sfizz now has fallen 45 dB.
- **SFZ ranges.** Velocity ranges start at 1. A velocity of 0 is a note-off. Bend ranges are in whole cents. The curves that move filter cutoffs and resonances with controllers keep their values within the documented range. The README lists the opcodes whose values can still lie outside the documented ranges.

## Other changes

- **SFZ.** The README names sfizz 1.2 as the player tested. It says that the opcodes follow their documentation at sfzformat.com. A new list gives the exceptions: decays and releases longer than 100 s, volumes above 6 dB, resonances below 0 dB, biquad type 5's resonance amount of 50 dB and cutoffs above half the output's sample rate. The list of sfizz 1.2.3's quirks adds its quicker decays and releases.
- **SoundFont players.** The README no longer says that the SoundFonts target FluidSynth. It names the players' versions where they differ. In FluidSynth before 2.4.0, a tremolo stays below full level. FluidSynth 2.4.3 and later cut key-group notes in about 0.3 s. BASSMIDI 2.4.16 ignores the CC1 and CC102 modulators and the modulator without a source. A new bullet says that hacsf2 writes the biquad low-pass's resonance for FluidSynth, BASSMIDI and the E-mu chips. They play a resonance of r dB as r - 3 dB at the cutoff.
- **The libraries.** hacsf2 now builds on seqlib 1.3.0, xsflib 1.5.0 and ntdlib 1.11.0.

## Known limitations
